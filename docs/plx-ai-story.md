# Version 1 - Original Story

# Opening Pitch – PLxAI Engineering Intelligence Platform

Before we jump into the demo, let me frame why PLxAI matters.

Across engineering organizations, the challenge is no longer lack of data. It is the opposite.

Design teams are surrounded by specifications, drawings, DFMEA sheets, DVP plans, standards, reports, and tool-specific logs. But this knowledge is fragmented, inconsistent, and often trapped in tribal experience.

So even strong teams lose time in the same places:
- manual validation loops,
- repeated interpretation of standards,
- version-to-version comparison errors,
- and delayed decisions because the right context is hard to assemble quickly.

At LTTS, we built PLxAI to solve exactly this problem.

Not as another isolated AI assistant, but as an engineering intelligence platform that works across the Product Development Lifecycle.

PLxAI brings together multimodal understanding, enterprise retrieval, agentic orchestration, and human-in-loop governance in one system.

In practical terms, that means:
- ingesting engineering data from documents, tables, and drawings,
- grounding responses using vector and graph-based context,
- generating structured outputs like DFMEA and DVP artifacts,
- validating with citations and approval controls,
- and integrating with existing PLM and 3D ecosystems across cloud, private, or on-prem environments.

One of the most compelling proofs is the wire harness clash analysis use case.

Traditionally, teams face nearly 90% false positives in automated clash detection, which forces heavy manual filtering.

With PLxAI, CV, ML, and LLM reasoning are combined to classify clashes and prioritize severity, reducing manual review effort by up to 90% and compressing cycle time from weeks to hours.

And this is not a single-point success.

The platform already reflects enterprise momentum:
- 24 use cases in production,
- 16 in progress,
- 35+ identified for development,
- and 50+ customer engagements.

From an impact perspective, the results are clear:
- 15% to 20% reduction in engineering effort in current implementations,
- stronger DFMEA completeness and audit readiness,
- and a roadmap to scale benefits across programs over a 6 to 24 month horizon.

This is the shift PLxAI enables:
from disconnected AI experiments and manual engineering bottlenecks
to a scalable, governed AI capability embedded into day-to-day product development.

So this demo is not just about features.

It is about showing how teams can turn fragmented engineering knowledge into actionable intelligence, improve decision quality, and accelerate delivery across the full PDLC.

## 60-Second Demo Intro

PLxAI is our engineering intelligence platform for the Product Development Lifecycle.

In most organizations, engineering data is everywhere but usable intelligence is hard to access.
Teams spend too much time in manual validation, repeated standards interpretation, and version comparison cycles.

PLxAI unifies this with an AI-driven system that ingests multimodal engineering content, grounds responses using enterprise retrieval, and generates structured outputs such as DFMEA and DVP artifacts with governance controls.

The business impact is measurable: 15% to 20% engineering effort reduction in current implementations, and up to 90% manual review reduction in use cases like wire harness clash analysis.

With 24 production use cases, 16 in progress, and 35+ identified, PLxAI is moving teams from isolated AI experiments to scalable, production-ready engineering AI.

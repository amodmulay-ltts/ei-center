# Version 1 - Original Story

# Opening Pitch - AITest Autonomous Test Engineering

Before we go into the demo flow, let me frame why AITest is important.

In most engineering organizations, testing is still one of the biggest delivery bottlenecks.

Requirements arrive from multiple systems, test logic gets interpreted differently across teams, script creation is manual and repetitive, and execution feedback arrives too late to influence early decisions.

So while product development is accelerating, validation often remains fragmented.

That is the gap AITest is designed to close.

AITest is not just another script generator.
It is an orchestrated AI test engineering workflow that connects requirement understanding, test intelligence, procedure generation, automation scripting, and execution into a single lifecycle.

The flow starts by ingesting requirements from sources like Jira, DOORS, and documents.
Then an intelligence layer uses LLM + RAG reasoning to infer intent, identify edge cases, and strengthen coverage.
From there, procedures are generated as clear and reusable test steps with preconditions and expected outcomes.
Those procedures are then converted into executable artifacts in Python, CAPL, and Robot.
Finally, execution runs in HIL/SIL environments and feeds results back quickly for iterative improvement.

One of the strongest aspects of AITest is explainability during execution.

As each stage is selected in the demo, the detail panel explains what that stage does and the business value it contributes.
That makes the platform easy to communicate to both technical reviewers and business stakeholders in the same conversation.

And the value is cumulative across the chain, not isolated to a single step.

Automation in script generation and execution drives immediate speed gains,
while AI-driven requirement understanding and test intelligence improve quality upstream and reduce downstream rework.

This is the shift AITest enables:
from manual, tool-fragmented test engineering
to a scalable AI-orchestrated validation capability.

So this demo is not just about generating tests faster.
It is about delivering better release confidence with stronger traceability, consistent quality, and a repeatable approach to autonomous test engineering.

The workflow architecture is presented in five connected stages.

1. Requirements
We ingest Jira, DOORS, and PDF inputs into a machine-readable model with semantic mapping and traceability anchors.
Business value: shrink manual requirement-to-test mapping effort by about 90%.

2. Intelligence
RAG + LLM reasoning maps constraints, edge scenarios, and safety-critical conditions to validated domain templates.
Business value: stronger standards alignment and more relevant test coverage for ISO 26262 workflows.

3. Procedures
We generate deterministic procedures with preconditions, signal expectations, and explicit pass/fail oracles.
Business value: a shared and reusable test language across teams and suppliers.

4. Script Generation
Procedures are transformed into executable assets in Python, CAPL, and Robot, ready for CI and simulation benches.
Business value: near zero-touch script authoring for repetitive coverage paths.

5. Execution
Scripts are routed to HIL/SIL targets, telemetry is captured, and outcomes feed continuous model improvement.
Business value: a fast and measurable feedback cycle for release readiness.

ReqSpec - Requirements to Specifications
Accelerate Specification Creation. Align Stakeholders. Transform Requirements Engineering.

What Is ReqSpec?
ReqSpec is an AI-powered tool that transforms informal, natural-language requirements into formal software specifications and professional UML diagrams. It integrates seamlessly into environments such as VS Code (and is extendable into MPS/CARIAD Archi), helping teams bridge business intent and engineering-grade documentation without disrupting existing workflows.
It's designed to be a starting point for procurement, process, and engineering teams or anyone with ideas, where a single description of "what to build" automatically generates both the structured specifications or business flows that procurement and compliance teams need and the technical diagrams that engineers require for implementation planning.
Key Capabilities That Drive Impact
1. Natural Language to Structured Specifications
Turn plain input into consistent, reviewable specs - fast.

•	Plain-language input: Requirements can be written in plain English, lowering barriers for non-technical stakeholders while maintaining the rigor engineers need. No specialized terminology required to get started.
•	Template-driven completeness: Automatically generates structured specifications including user stories, acceptance criteria, functional and non-functional requirements, key entities and relationships, edge cases, and success criteria.
•	Standards-aligned output: Generated specifications follow enforced templates and standards, ensuring consistency across teams and reducing the back-and-forth typical of traditional requirement review cycles.
2. UML Diagrams for Technical Clarity
Make architecture discussions concrete with diagrams derived from the same source input.
•	Multi-diagram generation: Supports class, sequence, state machine, and package diagrams, with the flexibility to extend to additional diagram types as your needs evolve.
•	Single source of truth: From one natural-language requirement description, ReqSpec produces both structured specifications and corresponding UML models, eliminating translation gaps between what was specified and what was designed.
•	Architecture-aware modeling: Diagrams respect your actual system boundaries and naming conventions, keeping technical discussions grounded in reality rather than generic examples.
3. Governed Output and Local Execution
Keep results predictable by grounding AI in your domain and keep control over where data is processed.

•	Contextual grounding: ReqSpec pulls relevant domain knowledge, subsystem definitions, interfaces, legacy baselines, approved terminology, so the AI operates within your actual architecture, not generically.
•	Local and on premises deployment: Fully on premises processing, air gapped compatibility, and no external API exposure. The tool can run entirely locally with no network connection required, ensuring data never leaves your infrastructure.
•	Seamless toolchain integration: Delivered as a VS Code plugin that works within existing developer workflows, reducing friction and enabling adoption without process disruption.
Business Value: Strategic, Measurable, Immediate
Reduce time spent on documentation-heavy work
In typical engineering organizations, a significant share of requirement effort (70–80%) is spent on manual writing, formatting, and structuring, leaving only 20–30% for solving the actual problem. ReqSpec flips this dynamic: AI-assisted structuring reduces requirement preparation time by roughly 60 to 70%, generating initial structured drafts in minutes instead of hours.
Create stronger alignment across procurement and engineering
By converting natural-language ideas into consistent specifications and diagrams, ReqSpec improves communication between process-driven stakeholders and technical teams. Procurement teams get the structured specs needed for vendor alignment and compliance documentation; engineers get the diagrams needed for architecture decisions, all from one source of truth.
Illustrative capacity impact
In a team of 10 engineers spending 8 hours per feature across 100 features annually (800 hours), even a 50% reduction in documentation effort frees approximately 400 engineering hours, unlocking capacity for additional features or innovation work with no added headcount.
How will this solution help you?
If your organization needs both process-driven stakeholders (procurement, compliance, process owners) and technical teams (engineers, architects) to work from a single, consistent definition of requirements, ReqSpec bridges that gap. By converting plain-language descriptions into structured specifications and UML diagrams from one source, it reduces iterations, improves alignment, and frees technical capacity for design work rather than documentation maintenance.
With local deployment options and seamless VS Code integration, it fits into existing workflows while eliminating the need for external portals or network dependencies.
